# Operations Handover & Final Closeout
**Target Date: 3 February 2027 (Task T28)**

## 1. Handover Scope & Objectives
Transfer complete administrative, operational, and technical control of the LoneWolf Sportswear Platform to the Business Owner and designated operations staff.

## 2. Transfer Checklist
- [ ] **Catalog & Inventory Training**: Owner trained on creating products, editing sizes/colors, and updating stock quantities.
- [ ] **Order Fulfillment Training**: Owner trained on processing incoming orders, verifying payment settlement, and assigning courier resi.
- [ ] **Domain & DNS Ownership**: Cloudflare account ownership and DNS records transferred.
- [ ] **Hosting & Secret Ownership**: Vercel / Railway project admin access transferred; billing attached to Owner's corporate card.
- [ ] **Database & Backup Ownership**: Managed PostgreSQL dashboard transferred; automated snapshot schedule verified.
- [ ] **Payment & Courier Accounts**: Midtrans / Xendit and Biteship live merchant credentials confirmed under Owner's legal entity.
- [ ] **Runbooks Delivery**: All runbooks (`docs/*`) delivered in clean Markdown and shared via secured drive.
- [ ] **Final Sign-off**: Handover document signed by both parties, concluding the 1-month babysitting window.
